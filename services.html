DED / DET Company Licence Process in Dubai

Get Your Dubai Mainland Trade Licence. The Dubai Department of Economy and Tourism (DET), formerly known as DED, handles licensing and regulation for many Dubai mainland businesses. Profentra Aurex assists businesses with the Dubai mainland company licence process, from activity selection through licence issuance.

DED / DET Process

01

Select Business Activity

Determine the activity or activities your company intends to conduct.

02

Select Legal Form

Choose the applicable legal structure based on the activity and ownership requirements.

03

Reserve Trade Name

Submit your preferred company name for reservation and approval.

04

Initial Approval

Apply for the required initial approval.

05

MOA & Documents

Prepare the applicable incorporation documents and Memorandum of Association where required.

06

Tenancy / Ejari

Complete the applicable office and tenancy requirements.

07

External Approvals

If your activity is regulated, obtain approvals from the relevant authority.

08

Submit Final Application

Submit the required documents and approvals for final licence processing.

09

Pay Government Fees

Complete the applicable government and licensing payments.

10

Receive Your Trade Licence

Once approved, your Dubai mainland trade licence is issued.
